Hapus akun & data DigitalDesk

Aplikasi Android DigitalDesk (com.digitaldesk.etc) · Pengembang: Ibra.etc

DigitalDesk tidak memiliki server dan tidak membuat akun sendiri. "Akun" di aplikasi adalah login Google (OAuth) yang bersifat opsional. Data Anda tersimpan di HP Anda, atau langsung di Google Drive milik Anda.

A. Hapus akun (cabut login Google)

  1. Buka DigitalDesk → menu akun → Keluar. Izin Google untuk DigitalDesk langsung dicabut.
  2. Atau buka myaccount.google.com/permissions → pilih DigitalDesk → Hapus semua akses.

B. Hapus sebagian data tanpa menghapus akun

  • Link, daftar Drive, Quick Access: hapus item dari dalam aplikasi.
  • Hasil scan & cadangan Riwayat versi: hapus file di folder Documents/Digital Desk di HP.
  • Salinan Google Sheets (cache): Pengaturan HP → Aplikasi → DigitalDesk → Penyimpanan → Hapus data.
  • ID iklan: Pengaturan HP → Google → Iklan → reset/hapus ID iklan.
  • Backup di Google Drive (bila diaktifkan): matikan di Setting → Backup ke Google Drive, lalu hapus folder “DigitalDesk Backup” di Google Drive Anda.

C. Hapus semua data

Uninstall DigitalDesk. Semua data di penyimpanan internal aplikasi langsung terhapus. Cadangan pengaturan Android (Auto Backup) bisa dihapus di Pengaturan HP → Google → Cadangan.

Data yang dihapus & disimpan

  • Dihapus segera: token login, email akun yang tersimpan, cache sheet, link, dan pengaturan di HP.
  • Tidak dihapus: file Google Sheets milik Anda di Google Drive (milik Anda, bukan data aplikasi), dan file di Documents/Digital Desk sampai Anda menghapusnya sendiri.
  • Pengembang tidak menyimpan data Anda di server mana pun. Data iklan/analitik dikelola Google (AdMob, Firebase) sesuai masa retensi Google; data Analytics disimpan maksimal 14 bulan.

Delete your DigitalDesk account & data

Android app DigitalDesk (com.digitaldesk.etc) · Developer: Ibra.etc

DigitalDesk has no server and does not create its own accounts. The "account" in the app is an optional Google sign-in (OAuth). Your data lives on your phone, or directly in your own Google Drive.

A. Delete account (revoke Google sign-in)

  1. Open DigitalDesk → account menu → Sign out. DigitalDesk's Google access is revoked immediately.
  2. Or go to myaccount.google.com/permissions → choose DigitalDesk → Delete all access.

B. Delete some data without deleting the account

  • Links, Drive list, Quick Access: remove items inside the app.
  • Scans & version-history backups: delete files in the Documents/Digital Desk folder.
  • Cached Google Sheets: Phone Settings → Apps → DigitalDesk → Storage → Clear data.
  • Advertising ID: Phone Settings → Google → Ads → reset/delete advertising ID.
  • Google Drive backups (when switched on): switch off in Settings → Back up to Google Drive, then delete the “DigitalDesk Backup” folder in your Google Drive.

C. Delete all data

Uninstall DigitalDesk. All data in the app's internal storage is removed immediately. The Android settings backup (Auto Backup) can be deleted in Phone Settings → Google → Backup.

What is deleted & kept

  • Deleted immediately: sign-in token, saved account email, sheet cache, links and settings on the phone.
  • Not deleted: your own Google Sheets files in Google Drive (they are yours, not app data), and files in Documents/Digital Desk until you delete them.
  • The developer stores none of your data on any server. Ads/analytics data is managed by Google (AdMob, Firebase) under Google's retention periods; Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months.
